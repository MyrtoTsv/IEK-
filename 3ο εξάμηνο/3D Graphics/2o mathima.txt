>sto modify panel , cap segments 2 , height 36
>R me oti epeksergasia , panta se uniform
>deksi click ston kulindro ,convert to editable poly gia thn epeksergasia , me anoixto to R to uniform
>sto menu deksia , sto 2o pou einai to modify panel,pame sto modifier list ,sto editable poly
>Epilegoume to vertex, pairnoume sta duo panw ,R kai scale  kai ta kanoume scale (mikrainoume)
>epilegoume to panw ,meta me to move pshlwnoume to panw 
>meta edit , switft loop , vazoume mia loop , kai allo ena loop ligo pio katw
>Epilegoume ta duo panw kai ta kanoume scale pros ta eksw na fainetai san kapaki
>sto modifier list ,sto editable poly, patame to polygon
>pame sto stomio stou cylinder , me me control pathmeno , epilegoume ola ta polugwna panw ston mirko kuklo kai to kanoume delete
>Paw se edge mode,Se ola tis grammes orizonties ,deksi click, kanoume chamfer , anoigoiume to menu tou chamfer kai sto deutero , anevazw ton dekadiko arithmo mexri na stroggulopoihthei se diafora opws mou fainetai pio omorfo
>Katw katw , metakinoume me scale to katw kuklaki
>Kai prospathoume na to kanoume move pros ta panw ligo

Etiketa panw sto krasi:
>M  sto object , anoigei to materials editor , sto physical material vazw multisubobject material
>enai 10 , sto set number epilegw mono ta 2.Sto none to vgazw to none kai twra vazw physical material
>kai sta dyo vazw diaforetika xrwmata
>Meta to kanw drag and drop sto object
>Meta sto modifier list, paw sto polygon , epilegw olo to object kai vazw set ID 1, kai meta sto epilegw ta polygon pou thelw na valw thn etiketa me control , kai ta kanw set ID 2.
Ama leitourghsei swsta , tha exw duo diaforetika xrwmata
>Meta sto material editor , dipla apo to xrwma ths "etiketas",pataw to keno gkri tetragwnaki , kai psaxnw thn leksh bitmap.
>Vazw thn eikona pou exw katevasei kai thn fortwnei.Omws twra den xwraei sta polugwna kai prepei na thn kanoume adjust.

>Katw deksia ,sto All iDs,epilegw to 2 kai mias vgazei ta polugons ths etiketas
>kai sto menu panw aristera , pataw to trito pathmeno kai to patw kai pros ta panw na xwresei
